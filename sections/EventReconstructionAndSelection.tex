\section{Event Reconstruction and Selection}
\label{sec:event_selection}

\subsection{Object Definitions}
\label{sec:particle_categories}

The archived reconstruction builds energy-flow candidates from combined
tracking and calorimeter information. Charged particle candidates rely on high-quality tracking
measurements, while neutral particle candidates utilize the calorimeter. The objects are categorized with an integer label \texttt{pwflag} as summarized in Table~\ref{tab:pwflag_definition}.

\begin{table}[ht!]
\centering
\begin{tabular}{c l}
\hline
\texttt{pwflag} & Category \\
\hline
0 & charged hadrons \\
1 & electrons (tracker + ECAL category) \\
2 & muons (tracker + muon category) \\
3 & $V^{0}$ candidates \\
4 & photons \\
5 & neutral hadrons \\
\hline
\end{tabular}
\caption{Object labels used in archived ALEPH events.}
\label{tab:pwflag_definition}
\end{table}

\noindent $V^0$ candidates (\texttt{pwflag}=3) include $K_S$ and $\Lambda$ from displaced-vertex reconstruction. These objects enter the thrust sum with their measured momentum vector rather than individual decay products, which is consistent with treating them as single hadronic objects for event-shape purposes. Their contribution to the thrust numerator and denominator is captured by the neutral-response systematic variations.

\subsection{Object and Event Selections}
\label{sec:object_selection}

Table~\ref{tab:particle_selection_summary} lists the object-level selection. These criteria define which reconstructed objects are propagated into thrust and therefore which detector effects enter the response model. They are imposed on the reconstructed collection only; the generator-level particles of the same events are retained without them. Here $d_0$ and $z_0$ are the transverse and longitudinal impact parameters of the track with respect to the per-event reconstructed primary vertex. Both are taken from the archived reconstruction. $N_{\mathrm{TPC}}$ is the number of TPC coordinates associated with the track.

\begin{table}[ht!]
\centering
\begin{tabular}{@{}p{0.37\textwidth}p{0.57\textwidth}@{}}
\hline
Object Variable & Requirement \\
\hline
Charged category & \texttt{pwflag}\,$\in[0,2]$ \\
Charged acceptance & $|\cos\theta|\le 0.94$ \\
Charged transverse momentum & $p_{\mathrm{T}}\ge 0.2$ GeV \\
Charged impact parameter & $|d_0|\le 2$ cm \\
Charged longitudinal impact & $|z_0|\le 10$ cm \\
Charged hit quality & $N_{\mathrm{TPC}}\ge 4$ \\
Neutral category & \texttt{pwflag}\,$\in[3,5]$ \\
Neutral energy & $E\ge 0.4$ GeV \\
Neutral acceptance & $|\cos\theta|\le 0.98$ \\
Use charged objects & \texttt{keepChargedTracks} = \texttt{True} \\
Use neutral objects & \texttt{keepNeutralTracks} = \texttt{True} \\
\hline
\end{tabular}
\caption{Object selections applied to data and detector level MC.}
\label{tab:particle_selection_summary}
\end{table}

The hadronic event selection is summarized in Table~\ref{tab:event_selection_summary}, following the standard ALEPH selection~\cite{Heister:2003aj, Barate:1996fi}. These selections enforce a phase space with high tracking quality, controlled forward acceptance, and stable visible-energy topology before entering event-shape reconstruction. The residual contamination from processes such as $e^+e^- \rightarrow \tau^+\tau^-$ is expected to be less than 0.26\% for these event selections~\cite{Barate:1996fi}. Approximately 1.36 (1.32) million $e^+e^-$ collisions resulting in the decay of a Z boson to quarks are analyzed (selected). Note that the acceptance requirement based on the event shape variable sphericity introduces a slight sculpting of the thrust distribution. The cut removes approximately 2.3\% of selected events, with the largest effect in the multijet region. In data, the ratio of the thrust distribution with the cut to that without rises monotonically from $\approx 0.85$ at $T \approx 0.6$--$0.75$ to $\approx 0.95$ at $T \approx 0.9$ and $\approx 0.99$ as $T \to 1$.

This yield has negligible effect on the thrust measurement and is covered within a systematic variation affecting the neutral particle acceptance.

\subsection{Data-MC Kinematic Checks}

The agreement between the data and MC samples is assessed below. The particle and track level distributions are shown after all selections. The energy-flow candidates are categorized as charged tracks (Figure~\ref{fig:pwflag0_full}), charged lepton 1 (electron) candidates (Figure~\ref{fig:pwflag1_full}), charged lepton 2 (muon) candidates (Figure~\ref{fig:pwflag2_full}), neutral long-lived particles that decay to two charged tracks ($V^{0}$) (Figure~\ref{fig:pwflag3_full}), photons (Figure~\ref{fig:pwflag4_full}), and neutral hadrons (Figure~\ref{fig:pwflag5_full}) based on the reconstructed properties utilizing different detector subsystems. Event-level variables used for hadronic selection are shown in Figure~\ref{fig:event_selection_observables}. The selections on these observables define the phase space used to build detector response and unfolded event-shape spectra. The data-MC agreement is in general quite good with some mismodeling observed in particular for the neutral particles.

Several effects are visible in these distributions. An enhancement near $\cos\theta=0$
appears in both data and simulation for charged tracks, as shown in Figure~\ref{fig:pwflag0_cosTheta}. It originates from the fixed floating-point precision used when the archived sample was reprocessed and will be addressed in a subsequent reprocessing. Narrow peaks at $E\simeq|\vec{p}|\simeq45$~GeV appear in the charged-track (Figures~\ref{fig:pwflag0_pmag},~\ref{fig:pwflag0_pt},~\ref{fig:pwflag0_energy}) and $V^{0}$ (Figures~\ref{fig:pwflag3_pmag},~\ref{fig:pwflag3_pt},~\ref{fig:pwflag3_energy}) spectra of the reconstructed simulation but not at generator level. These are reconstruction-level effects, and their origins remain under study. The muon $\phi$ spectrum in Figure~\ref{fig:pwflag2_phi} shows an enhancement present only at generator level, which is believed to arise from the processing of the archived simulation. The $\mathrm{N}_\mathrm{trk}$ oscillatory structure for the generator-level distributions in Figure~\ref{fig:ntrk} is an artifact of the following: charged particles are produced in pairs, so even bins are populated, while radiation effects populate odd bins. The impact of these effects on the thrust spectrum is negligible.
