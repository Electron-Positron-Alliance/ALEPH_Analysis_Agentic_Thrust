## 2. Figure Quality Issues (high priority)

### 2.1 Figure 21 (p34) — Overlapping title text on correlation matrix
The title reads "ALEPH e+e-, √s = 91.2 GeV" overlaid directly on top of "Total correlation matrix (final result)". The two text strings collide and are unreadable. This is the most visually broken figure in the note.

**Fix:** Move the ALEPH label to a `\text{}` annotation inside the canvas (e.g., top-left corner) or remove the duplicate matplotlib title, keeping only one identifier.

### 2.2 Figure 23 (p36) — Overlapping title text on charged-only comparison
Same issue as Figure 21: "ALEPH e+e-, √s = 91.2 GeV" overlaps with "Unfolded corrected (5 iters)" in the title area. The two strings are printed on top of each other.

**Fix:** Use `myText()` for the ALEPH label and set the ROOT canvas title separately, or remove the automatic histogram title.

### 2.3 Figure 9 (p17) — Phi distribution plots too small and noisy
The two phi-distribution panels are very small (each roughly 40% of text width). The y-axis label "Particles (normalized)" is barely readable. The MC gen (blue) line shows massive fluctuations with huge error bars dominating the visual. The HCAL module boundary vertical lines add clutter.

**Fix:** Increase figure width (use `\textwidth` instead of side-by-side), enlarge axis labels, and consider smoothing or rebinning the MC gen distribution if the fine-bin noise is not physics-relevant.

### 2.4 Figure 8 (p17) — pwflag=5 cosθ comparison noisy
The right panel ("data with vs without event selection") has large statistical fluctuations and the "Data (no sel)" dashed line is hard to distinguish. The yellow band marking the cleaning cut region is effective but the overall impression is noisy.

**Fix:** Consider rebinning or using a ratio panel to make the cut effect clearer.

### 2.5 Figures 2–7 (pp11–16) — 9-panel kinematic validation plots
These 3×3 grids are dense but functional. Issues:
- **Legend text is very small** — "MC GenBefore" label is nearly unreadable at print resolution
- **Mass panels** (bottom-right in each grid) show mostly empty distributions for charged categories with just a spike at zero; these panels add little information for pwflag=0,1,2
- **Ratio panels** are very compressed vertically — the MC/Data ratio information is squeezed into ~15% of the subplot height

**Fix:** Consider (a) increasing the figure size to full-page for at least pwflag=0 and pwflag=5 which are the most important categories, (b) enlarging legend text, (c) giving ratio panels more vertical space.

### 2.6 Figure 11 (p21) — Efficiency and miss-term plots lack axis titles
The left panel y-axis says "Reco efficiency (matched / all gen)" in small text that's partially cut off. The right panel has no clear y-axis title. Both panels use raw ROOT default styling without ALEPH header text.

**Fix:** Add consistent axis labels, ALEPH√s label, and match the style of other analysis figures.

### 2.7 Figure 19 (p31) — matplotlib vs ROOT style inconsistency
Figure 19 (per-bin uncertainty breakdown) is a matplotlib plot, while all other figures are ROOT-produced. The font, line style, legend box, and axis styling are visibly different. This breaks visual consistency.

**Fix:** Either regenerate this figure in ROOT to match the rest, or apply a ROOT-like matplotlib style (serif fonts, no grid, matching line widths).

### 2.8 Figure 22 (p34) — Final result panels are small
The two most important panels in the note (final unfolded spectrum + ALEPH comparison, and OmniFold/IBU comparison) are each only ~45% of text width. For the flagship result, these deserve more visual prominence.

**Fix:** Consider stacking them vertically (each at full width) or at minimum increasing to 0.85\textwidth side by side.

## 5. Physics Content Observations

### 5.1 Endpoint bin uncertainties
Table 8 shows full-range maximum uncertainties of 467.8% (experimental) and 343.2% (theory). While the text correctly notes these are endpoint-dominated, the note should consider whether reporting the full 0.50–1.00 range column is useful or whether it just draws attention to inherently unreliable bins.

### 5.2 Theory uncertainty dominance
In the central range, theory modeling (max 32.9%) is the largest single component. The note acknowledges this but doesn't discuss whether additional generator variations (e.g., Vincia, Dire) or direct NLO reweighting could reduce this. A brief discussion of potential reduction paths would strengthen the note.

### 5.3 ISR correction gap
Section 7.2 explicitly states the ISR correction is "not yet implemented." This is the largest known systematic gap relative to published ALEPH results and should be prominently flagged in the abstract or conclusion as a limitation of the current baseline.
