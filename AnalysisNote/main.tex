\abstract{
We present a measurement of the thrust distribution in $e^{+}e^{-}$ collisions at $\sqrt{s}=91.2$~GeV using archived ALEPH data, carried out entirely by AI agents (OpenAI Codex and Anthropic Claude) under expert physicist direction. 
A detector-corrected spectrum with full covariance is obtained via Iterative Bayesian Unfolding, suitable for precision QCD phenomenology. 
This work is a first step toward a theory--experiment loop in which AI agents perform rigorous measurements directly confrontable with AI-assisted theoretical calculations, accelerating the cycle that drives discovery in fundamental physics. 
Our work suggests that precision physics, leveraging the open LEP data and advanced theoretical calculations, provides an ideal testing ground for developing advanced AI systems for scientific applications.
}
